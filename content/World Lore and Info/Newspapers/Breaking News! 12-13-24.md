
### *In the newspapers...*
## Threat in West Eased Thanks to Sharn's Own Moonshine
### Troupe Received Approval from HM King Boranel.
-[['Nosy' Nancy Na'at]]

With the majority of Brelish forces deployed to the eastern borders to answer the threats posed by the militant "Lord of Blades", few forces remained in the West to content with an unexpected threat from the breakaway region of Droaam - an army advancing towards the southern end of the Graywall Mountains.

With the blessing of HM King Boranel, the proprietors of Lower Dura's own Moonshine clinic and shelter, took it upon themselves to assist our stretched defenses.  

Reports courtesy of House Sivis from the area confirm that Shadowlock Keep had fallen under attack by Droammi forces, which Moonshine helped repel. They then went on to rendezvous with a regional mining company operated by Porseth Torrn (d'Tharashk). Moonshine entered their silver mine, apparently sabotaged by Droaami forces, and while mining operations have not resumed, the Droaami army retreated back into their territory.  

Official sources embedded deep in Droaam are also reporting that the Sisters of Sora Kell, the upstart rulers of the breakaway territory, officially deny and disavow the military action, claiming its leaders will be punished for disobeying orders. Justice is brutal in Droaam, with little or no expectation of due process, but any imprisonment or execution of the rogue elements is a gain for Breland, in this reporter's opinion.  

Moonshine has returned safely to Sharn. HM King Boranel has announced his intention to award them each with a Distinguished Service medal.

House Tharashk has filed a complaint in court, claiming that, as the King's agents, Moonshine's failure to restore access to their mining operations constitutes a violation of their neutral status, as the silver in the mine was intended for trade with Droaam, and thus the move to leave it unusable was a politically partisan move.


## Self-declared 'Lord of Blades' to Make Official Visit to Wroat

-Lanwar Boldhelm, contributing journalist

According to a press statement released by HM King Boranel ir'Wynarn of Breland, the court will be receiving an official diplomatic mission from the warforged warlord known as the "Lord of Blades", who recently declared his nascent kingdom somewhere in the Mournlands, which occurred alongside his policy announcement of open immigration for warforged.

The Lord of Blades is scheduled to cross into Breland on Eyre *(April)* 28, and will be provided rail travel to Wroat with several layovers for security purposes, arriving in Wroat on Dravago *(May)* 1. The King will officially receive the Lord in his throne room, whereupon official diplomatic relations will begin. The King is expected to follow traditional Galifar custom and invite the Lord to enjoy his hospitality for at least one week. Tradition does not require the Lord to accept, though whether he is inclined to honor traditions at all is an unknown factor.

As is customary, diplomatic immunity will be extended to the Lord and his entourage, and any attacks upon his person will be treated as attacks upon the King himself.
