### 3: Overview:

I recommend reading the rules for Bastions yourself. The rules are on page 333 of the new DMG.

You gain a Bastion starting at level 5. This can be anything from a simple plot of land with a building on it, to a small keep, to a wizard tower, to your old family home, whatever you imagine.

Your Bastion has **Basic Facilities** and **Special Facilities**. Basic Facilities have no mechanical benefits and serve entirely immersion or role-playing purposes. Special Facilities provide you mechanical benefits. You can arrange your rooms in multiple stories acd :s you like - stairs and hallways are considered free.
  
You can combine your Bastion with other players' Bastions into a single structure, but you treat them separately - one player can only give Orders to their own Special Facilities.
