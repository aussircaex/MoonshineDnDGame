##### Walls
You can build a 20' wall around your Bastion. This can be abstracted however you like, rather than only being a wood or stone wall or similar. One 5' square costs 250gp and takes 10 days to build (concurrently if multiple squares are purchased). It only provides a mechanical benefit if the wall is complete, but you can partially build the wall as you go. If you renovate and expand your Bastion, you can move already purchased walls for free as part of the renovation.  
  
##### 2 Special Facilities from levels 5-8
As characters between level 5 and 8, you can have **2 Special Facilities**. You can switch one facility for another when you gain a level. Whenever the DM calls for a Bastion turn (usually every 7 days of game time unless a long time-skip happens), you can issue an Order to each of your Special Facilities. Each Facility lists what Orders can be issued, except that *all* Facilities can be given the **Maintain** Order. If you don't or can't provide an Order, they take the Maintain Order.
  
The **Maintain** Order has a 50% chance for nothing to happen, else can have good or bad events happen, from an attack on your Bastion to providing you free magical items like scrolls or Potions. Check page 350 in the DMG for what can happen.
  
You can only select a given Special Facility once unless it says it's repeatable.
  
Special Facilities are separate from whatever you have for Basic Facilities; if you have 20 squares of Basic Facilities and two Roomy Special Facilities, then your Bastion is 52 squares in size, plus hallways and staircases and such.  
  
Special Facilities are free, but some of them can optionally be expanded for a price in gold and time.

All Special Facilities come with appropriate staff, called hirelings. The Facility is assumed to generate enough income for their wages and maintenance for the Facility automatically. You are invited to come up with the individual names and basic descriptions of as many of your hirelings as you like.
  
At this tier, your options are:  
**Arcane Study**  
**Armory**  
**Barrack**  
**Garden**  
**Library**  
**Sanctuary**  
**Smithy**  
**Storehouse**  
**Workshop**  
  
###### Arcane Study
* Employs 1 hireling.
* You must be an Arcane caster to pick this facility (able to use an Arcane focus for your spells).  
* If you spend a Long Rest in your Bastion, you gain a Charm - the ability to cast Identify once for free in the next 7 days. You cannot gain more than one of this Charm at a time.  
* **Order: Craft an Arcane Focus**. Builds you an Arcane Focus for free after 7 days.  
* **Order: Craft a Book.** For 10gp, builds you a blank book (usable as a spellbook) after 7 days.
  
###### Armory
* Employs 1 hireling
* Stores weapons and armor for your Defenders (see Barrack below).
* **Order: Stock Armory**: Costs 100GP, plus 100gp per Defender. Upgrades their ability to defend. If your Bastion suffers an attack, your Armory is no longer stocked. Cost is halved if you have a Smithy.  
  
###### Barrack
* Repeatable
* Employs 1 hireling (in addition to Defenders)
* Houses Defenders. By default, can house 12 Defenders. Starts with 0 Defenders.
* **Order: Recruit**. After 7 days, add 4 Defenders up to cap.
* **Expand Facility**: pay 2000gp, takes 80 days. Increases cap to 25 Defenders.  
  
###### Garden
* Repeatable
* Employs 1 hireling
* Select a garden type. You can instruct your hireling to change from one type to another, which takes them 21 days.
	* Decorative
	* Food
	* Herb
	* Poison
* **Order: Harvest**. After 7 days, gain the output of your garden type:
	* Decorative: 50gp of decorative flowers OR 10 vials of perfume OR 10 candles.  
	* Food: 100 days' worth of Rations (that's a productive garden)  
	* Herb: 10 healer's kits or 1 Potion of Healing  
	* Poison: 2 vials of Antitoxin or 1 Basic Poison.  
* **Expand Facility**: pay 2000gp, takes 80 days. Adds a second garden to your Garden which can have the same or different crops.  
  
###### Library
* Employs 1 hireling
* **Order: Research Topical Lore:** After 7 days, your hireling acquires up to 3 pieces of information per the DM's discretion.
  
###### Sanctuary
* Employs 1 hireling.
* You must be able to use a Holy Symbol or Druidic Focus to cast spells in order to pick this facility.
* If you spend a Long Rest in your Bastion, you gain a Charm - the ability to cast *Healing Word* once for free in the next 7 days. You can have only one of this Charm at a time.
* **Order: Craft Sacred Focus:** After 7 days, your hirelings craft a Holy Symbol or Druidic Focus.  
  
###### Smithy
* Employs 2 hirelings
* Halves the cost of the Stock Armory Order of the Armory.
* **Order: Craft with Smith's Tools:** your 2 hirelings will make progress on crafting an item that requires Smith's Tools, per the rules in the PHB (see page 221 for Smith's Tools).
  
**Storehouse:**
* Employs 1 hireling
* **Order: Buy Goods:** Spend 500gp to fill the Storehouse with Trade Goods (see the PHB).
* **Order: Sell Goods:** If the Storehouse is full, sell off the goods for 550gp.

###### Workshop
* Employs 3 hirelings
* Pick 6 types of Artisan's Tools for your Workshop.
* Once per day, spending a Short Rest in your Workshop grants you Heroic Inspiration.
* **Order: Craft Adventuring Gear:** your 3 hirelings make progress on crafting adventuring gear, per the crafting rules in the PHB. They can only make items that the 6 selected types of Artisan's Tools can produce.
* **Expand Facility**: 2000gp, 80 days. Gain 2 more hirelings and 3 more types of Artisan's Tools.
