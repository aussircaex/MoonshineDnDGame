Basic Facilities are ordinary rooms like bedrooms, kitchens, courtyards, dining areas, and such. They come in three sizes: **Cramped** (4 squares), **Roomy** (16 squares) and **Vast** (36 squares). As written, the rules suggest that each one is a single kind of room, but Basic Facilities do not have any in-game benefit other than roleplaying purposes, so it doesn't matter to me how you divide up the space or "borrow" from other rooms.

Your Bastion starts with two facilities, one Cramped (4 squares), one Roomy (16 squares). This doesn't have to literally be two rooms, but it matters for buying new space (it's cheaper and faster per square to upgrade a facility).  
  
You can purchase a new Basic Facility or pay to upgrade existing ones to get more total space to suit your fancy. If you upgrade a facility, it takes the difference in gold cost and time. Projects all run concurrently.
  
New facility:  
Cramped (4 squares) New: 500gp, 25 days  
Roomy (16 squares) New: 1000gp, 45 days.
Vast (36 squares) New: 3000 gp, 125 days.

Upgrade an existing facility:
Cramped to Roomy: 500gp, 20 days.
Roomy to Vast: 2000 gp, 80 days.
  
As mentioned, this doesn't literally have to be one room per facility. Just total up the number of squares you need for your Bastion to figure out what it'll cost you to build.
