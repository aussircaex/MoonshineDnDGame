An order of assassins who fight using the magical powers of the Way of Shadow.

As far as the members of Moonshine have seen, their membership is entirely elven, but they are not known to be directly affiliated with the purported assassination wings of either [[Phiarlan - Shadow|House Phiarlan]] or [[Thuranni - Shadow|House Thuranni]].

The party have directly attacked a base of their operations in Sharn in the Fallen district in the lower city. They are also said to have a larger headquarters in Thronehold.
