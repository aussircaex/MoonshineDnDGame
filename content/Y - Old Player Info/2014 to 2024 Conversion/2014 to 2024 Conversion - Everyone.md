
This page compiles my notes for the rules update to the 2024 version of the game.

If you want to read a comprehensive guide online, there's a good one [At the website RPGbot, accessible through this link](https://rpgbot.net/dnd-2024-5e-transition-guide-and-change-log-everything-thats-different-in-the-new-players-handbook/).

You should be able to get the summary of the changes right here, though. We went over most of these changes in person last session, so most of these should be familiar to you.

# Rules Updates for Everyone

### Backgrounds
* Stat bonuses have been moved from race (now called species) to your Background.
	* **For our game**: Ignore this and leave your stats where they are, unless/until you make a new character.
* The new Backgrounds grant two specific skills, which may differ from what your 2014 Background gave you.
	* **For our game:** Ignore this, and leave your skill proficiencies where they are unless/until you make a new character.
* 2014 Backgrounds gave some combination of two languages or tool proficiencies. In the new version, all Backgrounds grant 1 tool proficiency and no languages, but the *base* number of languages a character knows has been increased from 2 to 3.
	* **For our game - languages:** If your character knows fewer than 3 languages, you can add languages until you have 3. If you gain extra languages from your class, such as Druidic or Thieves' Cant, that is in addition to your base of 3. Do not reduce the number of languages your character already knows if you have extras.
	* **For our game - tools:** If your character does not have at least 1 tool proficiency, you can gain one. If you have extra tool proficiencies from your class, such as Herbalism Kit or Thieves' Tools, that is in addition to your base of 1. Do not reduce the number of tool proficiencies your character already possesses if you have extras.
* 2014 Backgrounds granted a "Background Feature". New Backgrounds grant an "Origin Feat"
	* **For our game:** You lose your Background Feature, but you may select an Origin Feat of your choice. You do *not* need to take the specific Origin Feat associated with the new version of your Backround, if any.

### Spells
* A *lot* of spells have been changed or updated.
* For an exhaustive list of the changes, you can follow [This link here](https://rpgbot.net/dnd-2024-5e-transition-guide-and-change-log-everything-thats-different-in-the-new-players-handbook/#spell-changes).

### Other Mechanics
* The "**Surprise**" mechanic is different in 2024. In 2014, anyone who is Surprised loses their first turn in combat. In 2024, anyone who is Surprised has Disadvantage on their Initiative rolls and does not lose any turns.
* **Heroic Inspiration** (previous simply called Inspiration or "DM Inspiration") has been changed.
	* Rather than being spent proactively to gain Advantage on a roll, it can now be used reactively to reroll any one die (and not only a d20.)
	* If you gain Heroic Inspiration and you already have it, you can give it to someone else who does not have it.
	* **House Rule:** If you roll a natural 1 on a d20 test *and* fail, you gain Heroic Inspiration. Also, if you roll a natural 20 on a d20 test *and* succeed, you gain Heroic Inspiration.
	* Note that Humans now gain Heroic Inspiration at the end of every Long Rest.
* **Hit Dice Recovery:** You now regain *all* spent Hit Dice after a Long Rest - there's no longer a need to conserve them if you need healing after a Short Rest.
