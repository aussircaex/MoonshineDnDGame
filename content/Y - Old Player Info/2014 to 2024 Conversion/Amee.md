#### Rogue Updates

* **Weapon Proficiencies:** You are now proficient with Simple weapons and Martial weapons that have the Finesse or Light property.
	* This means you *lose* proficiency with the Longsword (which you weren't using anyway).
	* You retain proficiency with the rapier and shortsword.
	* You gain proficiency with the scimitar and whip.
* **Thieves' Cant:** You now get an **additional** language from the Thieves' Cant ability. This means your character should know at least 5 languages (Common, Thieves' Cant, and three others).
* **Weapon Mastery:** Pick two kinds of weapons. You can use the Mastery feature of these weapons. You can change your choices after a Long Rest.
	* The **Vex** property appears on shortbows and shortswords - if you have Mastery with a weapon and you hit with it, you have Advantage on your next attack against them until the end of your next turn.
	* The **Nick** property appears on daggers and scimitars - if you have Mastery with one of these, you can dual-wield in melee *without* using your Bonus Action to make your extra attack.
* **Cunning Strike:** This is a new ability you can use with your Sneak Attack. When you deal Sneak Attack damage, you can elect to do less damage to instead use special abilities! Check page 130 in the new PHB for the list.
* **Hiding in Combat**: This has been simplified; as long as you're out of any enemy's line of sight, it is a simple DC 15 check to Hide, otherwise, you are not hidden. You still need to know the result of your check if you succeed to know how difficult it for them to find you (e.g. if you rolled a 21, they must roll a 21 on Perception to find you).
