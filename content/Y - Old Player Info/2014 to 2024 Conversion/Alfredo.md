#### Cleric Updates

* The number of spells you can prepare is now fixed. See the chart on page 70.
* You now longer get weapon or armor proficiencies from your Twilight Domain. Instead, you have a new level 1 feature called **Divine Order**. Taking Protector will keep your proficiencies - let me know if you want to switch to Thaumaturge.
* **Channel Divinity:** You have more uses of Channel Divinity - see the chart on page 70, but only *one* use comes back on a Short Rest, rather than all of them.
* **Channel Divinity: Divine Spark:** In addition to Turn Undead, you have a new default Channel Divinity option - Divine Spark. Check page 70.
#### Druid Updates

* The number of spells you can prepare is fixed. See the chart on page 80.
* **Primal Order:** You get a new level 1 feature, Primal Order. If you pick Magician, make sure your cantrips known is correct. Page 80.
	* Since you likely already have Medium/Heavy armor training and Martial Weapons proficiency, I recommend selecting Magician.
* **Wild Shape**
	* Number of uses follows the chart on page 80. You get *one* use back on a Short Rest.
	* Wild Shape is now always a Bonus Action, even if you're not a Moon Druid.
	* Pick 4 animals you can transform into. CR 1/4 or lower. You can swap one for another after a Long Rest. They cannot have a Fly speed (until level 8 Druid), but they *can* have a Swim speed.
	* Instead of replacing your HP with the animal's, you get Temporary HP equal to your Druid levels.
	* You are *not* forced out of the animal form unless you are Incapacitated or die.
* **Wild Companion:** Your Wild Companion feature now allows you to use a spell slot instead of a Wild Shape use if you prefer, and the familiar lasts until you finish a Long Rest.

#### Circle of the Moon Updates

* The temporary HP you gain when you Wild Shape increases to your Druid level times three.
* Your AC in animal form is 13+WIS, which should help make up for the fewer bonus HP.
* You gain spells that you always have prepared - AND you can cast these (only these) spells while in animal form. Check page 86. Note that Starry Whip is a cantrip attack spell!
