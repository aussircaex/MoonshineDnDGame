#### Wizard Updates

* **Prepared Spells:** The number of spells you can prepare is now fixed per the chart on page 166 rather than being a function of level+INT.
* **Scholar:** You get to pick one skill from Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, or Religion that you have proficiency in to gain Expertise in.
* **Memorize Spell** You can swap one prepared level 1+ spell for another after a Short Rest.
