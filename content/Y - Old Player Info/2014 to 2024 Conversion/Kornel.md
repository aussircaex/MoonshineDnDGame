#### Sorcerer Updates
* **Prepared Spells:** The number of spells you know (now also referred to as "prepared") is increased - make sure you pick your new spells! Check the chart on page 140.
* **Innate Sorcery:** You have a brand-new ability. See page 140 for its rules.
* **Sorcerous Restoration:** You have another brand-new ability. See page 141 for its rules.
* Review the **Metamagic** options; they have all been altered slightly, mostly for the better (except Twinned Spell, which got a small nerf)

#### Aberrant Sorcery
* None of the abilities were changed, except the Psionic Spells can no longer be retrained.
