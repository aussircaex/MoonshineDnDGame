
Wandslingers are, simply, anyone who is trained to fight using damage-dealing spells. With highly exceptional individuals being exceptions, anyone who uses magic in Eberron requires a Focus to cast their spells, and wands are the most common implement used. As more and more soldiers were trained to use basic magic over the course of the [[The Last War|Last War]], the image of a wand-wielding soldier became iconic.

Most people trained as wandslingers do not have spell slots or spells of level 1 or higher, and so the most iconic part of their image is the use of damaging cantrips like like *Fire Bolt* and *Ray of Frost* that throw flashy projectiles.

Popular media like stage plays and magazine serials have created a popular costume for wandslingers, usually involving leather clothing or armor, duster jackets, and wide-brimmed hats (which vary depending on the place of origin and/or heroic/villainous disposition of the characters), such as cowboy hats or sombreros. Real-life wandslingers typically wore the uniforms and armors they were provided and trained to use, but pop culture afficionados continue to debate whether you can be a "real" wandslinger without the iconic look.
