On rare occasions, a phase spider will survive long enough to grow to an unusual size, with their ability to phase into and out of the Ethereal Plane growing as well, granting them a measure of control over the ground in their lairs as well.

The one encountered by the Moonshine Gang also displayed an uncanny resistance to the deleterious influence of [[Xoriat, the Realm of Madness|Xoriat]], but did not otherwise behave much differently than would be expected of a predatory animal defending its lair.
