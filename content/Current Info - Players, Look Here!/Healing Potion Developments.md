##### "Above table" / Not-in-universe rules changes:

**Rules update:** in the new 2024 version of the rules, you may use any Potion as a Bonus Action, whether drinking it or administering it to someone within reach. We will begin using this rule going forward. 

**House rule:** You may do so as an Action if you prefer, but can't use this house rule to use two potions on the same turn (unless you have an extra Action through Haste or Action Surge or something).

##### In-universe changes:

**Cannith Potion Breakthrough**: You were offered the chance to curry favor with House Jorasco by sabotaging Cannith's research into producing cheaper healing potions. You sided with Cannith, and their research bore fruit, though it will be a long time before the corporate politics and intrigue are resolved to sell them on a wide scale.  

While in Sharn, you can visit [[Cameron d'Cannith]] to purchase up to 10 Potions of Greater Healing for 50gp each (they cost 200gp elsewhere, if available at all).
