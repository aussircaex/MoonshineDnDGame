###### Burdlick visits the tower of Flamewind to study the Draconic Prophesy...

*To your great surprise, Flamewind the Sphinx is present when you go to visit her tower.*

"The river has flowed to its appointed point. From here, where it flows depends on you, Burdlick, student of Time."  

"Set before you is a task that no other has accomplished, and is far beyond even my capabilities. There will be an invocation of Chronomantic magic greater than any in all of time, from the beginning to the end. What happens when it does is up to you."

*Her eyes roll back in her head, revealing only white, and they begin glowing in their sockets as she utters the following:*

"When the child of land and sea becomes the master of ebb and flow,\
They shall stand before the Queen of Death, and the Queen shall behold the Heart of Orpheus.\
Her heart will be moved for but an instant.\
She shall grant its holder the power to change one thing in the past and future.\
Never again shall the Queen's heart be moved."

*Her eyes return to normal.*  

"There are few students of Chronurgy in this world, Burdlick, and fewer still that could qualify as a 'child of land and sea'. There are many who will try to find this Heart of Orpheus to ensure that they are granted their wish. But if it is you that is spoken of, then your death is one way this portion of the Draconic Prophesy can come to pass. There are many in this world who will desire that very outcome if it benefits them."

"You must choose how you wish the river to flow, Burdlick. We will not meet again."

*She then launches herself to the domed roof of her library, and takes wing, exiting through its skylight. Out the window, you can see her flying south, slowly disappearing over the horizon.*
