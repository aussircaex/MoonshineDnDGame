*A letter arrives in the mail for Bax. The return address is from your parents' bakery...*

Dear Bax,

We saw your name in the papers!! Your father and I were so proud! I bought extra copies so we could clip out the article and put it around the bakery so everyone can see it. On the front door, by the register, on the bulletin board...

I have some unfortunate news. Barney - you remember Barney, right? The charming boy with the handsome blue tiger pattern. He comes by asking about you every so often. I keep telling him you'll be back to work with us one of these days. Anytime you want to drop by the bakery, I'd be happy to let him know you'll be here. He's such a nice boy - he's studying to be an attorney, you know, and is specializing in Five Nations law, a *very* lucrative field, I'm told.

Oh, right. Barney visited the Gold Dragon Inn while traveling to Thronehold, and they're advertising a new cookie... he's pretty confident it's our family's recipe. Whoever stole our recipe, House Ghallanda has it now, and that means we'll the secret's out.

I know you've been going to some lengths to try to get the recipe back for us. I hope you'll still do that - it's more than just the recipe. Between you and me, your grandmother's recipe book itself is part of the secret (don't worry that I wrote this in the letter, the human girl who took the letter said it was completely confidential!)

Come visit us sometime! Your father and I miss you - and let us know when you'll be coming so I can tell Barney.

Love,
Mom (your father says hello too)
