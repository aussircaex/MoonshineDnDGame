###### In the middle of the night, during a peaceful sleep, Kuro experiences a vivid dream...

*You find yourself in a familiar-feeling place. In a dense forest, you walk into a small clearing. There is a Harengon man sitting at a campfire. The night is clear and bright, and Zarantyr - the moon on which the image of the Moon Rabbit can be seen, is full and clear. The man is dressed in a style that reminds you of Elder Fiver, a spiritual leader and magic instructor from your home in the Eldeen Reaches, but unlike Elder Fiver, he wears brightly colored feathers in a headdress, of red, yellow, and green colors. He gestures for you to sit by the fire, which you do.*  
  
"Hello, Kuro. I'm glad to finally meet you. It took me a long time to find you. And before you ask, no, I'm not the Moon Rabbit" *he says, gesturing at the sky before taking a moment to stir a pot of stew over the fire.* "Though I can say I've had the pleasure to meet her."  
  
"I apologize for intruding on your mind to enter your dream. I have few ways of contacting others. I know you must have many questions for me, but there's very little I can reveal to you - my enemies have eyes and ears everywhere, and not even dreams are safe from some of them. Even telling you my name could jeopardize me."  
  
"What I can tell you is that I am a friend, and I am dedicated to holding a great evil at bay. But my hold is slipping, and I need your help. So know this:"

*The man turns towards you, and all except his face seem to fade to blackness - no matter what happens, you know you will remember these words:*
  
"In twin cities, wrecked by hubris and rage,  
Each of which marked the freedom of the slaves,  
Lie shards of he who has always been dead.  
Take one to the source, and one to the mouth.  
For Nothing can quell the Rage of War."

*Your normal vision returns, and the man goes back to tending the fire.*
  
"Know that merely by revealing this to you, I have set in motion events that could spell my own doom. But my enemies move slowly; their plans take can take years to develop. But they will respond when they learn of your actions. So act patiently, with prudence, and with secrecy. Do not hesitate to fight for any cause you deem just along the way - if necessary, pass this quest along to your descendants. But know that if we do nothing, my prisoner will be free."  
  
"I will contact you in this way if I am able, but it may be a long time before I do. Blessings of the Moon be upon you, Kuro. May you never go hungry on the road."

*As he offers the familiar blessing of the faithful of the Moon Rabbit, the dream fades, and after an unknown amount of time sleeping, the morning arrives, and you feel very well-rested.*
