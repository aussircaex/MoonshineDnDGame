[[Happy Ben]], the talking capybara merchant, has unique items he trades for from all over Khorvaire. Note that any of these magic items may have additional minor effects not listed here that will only become apparent after purchase (this is not a setup for a prank on you).

If you are interested in finding specific magic items not sold by Happy Ben, you can use the "Purchasing a Magic Item" downtime rules to try to find them.

### **Inventory:**

**Feather Tokens:** These are the ordinary magic items sold all over Sharn.
Price: 25gp each.

**Pot of Awakening** *(common)*:
If you plant an ordinary shrub in this 10-pound clay pot and let it grow for 30 days, the shrub magically transforms into an awakened shrub at the end of that time. When the shrub awakens, its roots break the pot, destroying it. The awakened shrub is friendly toward you. Absent commands from you, it does nothing.
Price: 50gp (2 available).

### **No Longer Available:**

**Claymore of Pacifism** *(greatsword; uncommon)*:
When you wield this greatsword, your desire for violent conflict is eased. You suffer a -1 penalty to attack rolls and damage rolls you make with it, and any critical hit you inflict with it becomes a normal hit.
Price: 450gp.

**Rapier Wit** *(rapier; uncommon, requires attunement)*:
You may add your Charisma bonus instead of your Strength or Dexterity bonus to attack and damage rolls you make with this weapon. In addition, you can cast the Vicious Mockery cantrip while attuned to it and can use it as a spellcasting focus for that spell.
Price: 425gp.

**Cursed Shell Game Set** *(common)*:
This is a set of three shells and a token. When you play the shell game with this set, it is cursed, and the token always appears under one of the three shells, regardless of any attempt to remove it from play.
Price: 50gp.

**Ring of Beards** *(common)*:
While wearing this ring, you can grow facial hair if you are normally unable to do so, or you can halt your facial hair's growth if you normally grow it. This unusual ring does not count against the normal limit of two magical rings you can wear.
Price: 25gp.
