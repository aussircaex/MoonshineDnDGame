A fixer for [[Cannith - Making|House Cannith]]. Cameron offered you the opportunity to ally with House Cannith against the Boromar Clan.

Thanks to the party's efforts to assist House Cannith, they have made a breakthrough in the development of healing potions at a cheaper cost. Periodically (once per adventure), Cameron will sell up to ten Potions of Greater Healing to you at a cost of only 50gp.

Cameron uses they/them pronouns.
