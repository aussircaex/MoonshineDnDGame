Hruit is the Sharn City Councilor representing Middle Dura. He is a giant owl - not a humanoid like an aarakocra, but a true member of the noctural raptor bird species. Giant owls have always possessed the ability to speak in their own tongue, but are not usually capable of speaking humanoid language.

Hruit, for his part, is capable of enough druidic magic to shapeshift into a humanoid form - usually a [[Shifters|shifter]] - to interact with the people of Sharn. He has lived in the city for years, once competing in the city's famous sky races. He retired from the sport after questions of fairness were raised since other jockeys were riding non-sapient mounts, and then went on to run for political office.

He has long butted heads with [[Ilyra Boromar]], as he is a symbol of Sharn's cosmopolitan diversity and its immigrant population, contrasted with Ilyra, who represents the city's longstanding population, especially the Boromar Clan.
