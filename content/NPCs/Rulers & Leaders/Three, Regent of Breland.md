
Three was once a common warforged fighter in the Brelish army, until he fought beside [[King Boranel of Breland (†)|King Boranel]] and quickly became a trusted companion to him. He then became the King's bodyguard and personal valet.

Following the assassination of the King and most of his children, Three declared himself Regent of Breland while the surviving rightful heir was located. This has been met with significant controversy .
