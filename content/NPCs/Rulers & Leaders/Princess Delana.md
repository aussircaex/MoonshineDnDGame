One of [[King Boranel of Breland (†)|King Boranel]]'s many children, Princess Delana's life has never been followed closely by the press. She was disabled in a fire at an early age, and later, when the Last War ended, she was sent to live and study in Aundair, and largely did not participate in official public events.

After the assassination of King Boranel and most of his children, Delana was identified as the sole survivor among his children. The Moonshine Gang made it their mission to locate her and ensure her safety. Upon finding her, however, she reacted poorly to the news that the party was authorized by [[Three, Regent of Breland|Three]] and continued her flight into the [[6 - The Eldeen Reaches|Eldeen Reaches]].
