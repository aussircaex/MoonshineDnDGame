Thexan is a Mind Flayer, also called an Illithid, or a *Ghaik* by the Githyanki. Mind Flayers are aberrations, and are native to the plane of Xoriat, the Realm of Madness. Many of them serve the mighty daelkyr, but others are independent, and long ago dominated entire societies to serve them as food and slaves.

Their most notable victims were the Gith, who eventually rebelled and freed themselves from bondage. The Githyanki are a society of Gith who aggressively hunt Mind Flayers to this day, while the Githerzai are a society who seek to live a more peaceful existence - though with no shortage of training to defend themselves against Mind Flayers.

Thexan used his psychic powers to take a Droaami military leader under his thrall to lead an army to the border between Droaam and Breland. He sought the lost city of Siltanak, and while he did discover it, he was ambushed by a hunting party of Githyanki and forced to flee into the mine with only a small squad of bodyguards.  

Thexan encountered the party in Siltanak, the ancient Dhakaani city under the Graywall Mountains, while the party searched for a chunk of Pure Byeshk for study. He offered safe refuge while they conducted their search, only to turn on them and attempt to seize the Pure Byeshk. Thexan was defeated and fled using his *plane shift* power, but his guards were defeated, and the Droaami army retreated back into their territory.
