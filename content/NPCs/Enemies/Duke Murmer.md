A powerful demon of Khyber, Murmer appears as a vulture-like humanoid. Also known as the Demon of Cremation, the entrance his realm lay deep in the lava caverns below Sharn. His domain took the form of a valley carpeted with the bodies of the dead. A stone fortress stood in the center, which resembled a crematorium smokestack.

Moonshine traveled through his realm on their way to find the Oracle of the Shadow. Murmer bargained with the party, agreeing to safe passage through his domain in exchange for his freedom - they had only to bring him with them back to Eberron.

At the last moment, the party cast Murmer back into his domain. Enraged, he summoned his full power to attempt to force open the portal to Eberron. Only thanks to the party's intervention and the magical power of the Silver Flame was the demon sealed once more.
