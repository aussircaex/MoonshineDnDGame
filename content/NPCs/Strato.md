
A friendly cloud giant the party met on his three-story (by giant standards, so about 120 feet high) stone house on his flying cloud. Strato is a guest to Khorvaire, having traveled across the sea to visit the Aundairian university of Arcanix. He agreed to allow airships to use his cloud as a rendezvous point in exchange for permission to travel through Brelish airspace.

The party helped Strato to get rid of a problem - manticores were trying to get into his house and kill his beloved pet (giant) goose! As a reward, he gave the party a [[Sulatari Sword]], a weapon too too small to be of use to him except as a pocket knife.
