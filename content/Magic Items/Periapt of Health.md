*Wondrous item, uncommon* 

**Effect:** You are immune to contracting any disease while you wear this pendant. If you are already infected with a disease, the effects of the disease are suppressed while you wear the pendant.

The Dhakaani did not train many divine spellcasters and lacked Jorasco's healing abilities, so magical cures were almost unheard of. This necklace was likely made to relieve the suffering of important people who contracted chronic or terminal disease.
