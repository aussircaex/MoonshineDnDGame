*Weapon (battleaxe or handaxe), uncommon*

Named Human-Chopper by the smiths of Droaam, this wicked battleaxe is carved from byeshk, a rare material found in the eponymous Byeshk Mountains.

**Effect: +1**: Human-chopper is a +1 weapon, so you gain +1 to attack rolls and damage rolls you make with it.

**Effect: Byeshk**: Certain creatures are vulnerable to byeshk the same way that werewolves are vulnerable to silver.

**Effect: Human Bane**: Human-chopper deals an extra 1d6 damage against human targets. This trait does not function against half-elves, half-orcs, or similar targets.

**Effect: For All Goblins:** Human-chopper can be wielded as a battleaxe or as a handaxe. You can change how you wield it so long as you are not currently in combat.

**Detail:** This axe was made by and for goblins. Text along the hilt reads “Droaam is the heir of Dhakaan”.  While holding the axe, you can feel the hatred its creators felt against humans.
