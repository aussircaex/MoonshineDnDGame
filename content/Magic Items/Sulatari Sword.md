*Weapon (shortsword), uncommon*

This style of magical sword was once produced by the Sulatar, one of the major cultures of dark elves on Xen’drik who focus on their practice of fire magic. It was forged from magma pulled from Fernia itself. How Strato the Cloud Giant came to possess one isn’t clear.

**Effect: +1**: The Sulatari Sword is a +1 weapon, so you gain +1 to attack rolls and damage rolls you make with it.

**Effect: Fernian obsidian**: This weapon is made of stone, not metal, for the purposes of effects that target or affect certain materials.

**Effect:** Torch: As a bonus action, you can say the Elvish word for “light” and cause the sword to flicker with flames, shedding bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet. You can use a bonus action to end this effect. Sheathing the sword also ends the effect.

**Detail**: Peering into the volcanic glass the sword is forged with shows flickers of light and motion; possibly the shadows of events occurring somewhere on Fernia. Nothing is coherent enough about the images to interpret any meaning.
