*Shortsword*

This sword shimmers and is semi-transparent, as though it is not fully present on this plane of existence.

**Effect:** +1: The *phase blade* is a +1 weapon, so you gain +1 to attack rolls and damage rolls you make with it.

**Effect: Runes.** When you damage a creature that is suffering from madness or a curse with the *phase blade*, it is burned by its magic for one hour. If the creature dies in that time, the sword absorbs the madness, and it gains a strange rune along its blade.

*As you wield this blade, you recall a memory you did not experience. A voice softly laughs and says “is it madness, or do you simply understand what others cannot?”*
